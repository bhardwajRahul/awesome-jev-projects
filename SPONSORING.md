## Who It Fits

Awesome Jev is an independent, source-reviewed directory of Jev integrations, Agent pipelines, and related open-source projects. Ideal partners include:

- **Browser & Agent Automation Infrastructure**
- **Model Gateways, Routers & Optimization APIs**
- **Developer Experience, Debugging & Benchmarking Tools**

Every placement should answer a concrete question for developers: **What does this tool help them accomplish, and where can they immediately try it?**

The catalog currently features **260 curated project records** (253 source-reviewed entries and 7 explicitly marked as review-pending). This represents verified open-source coverage. Source inspection establishes architectural alignment, not production guarantees or traffic numbers. See our [catalog audit](docs/catalog-review-2026-09-19.md).

## Collaboration Workflow

1. **Send a brief**: Share your project URL, a one-sentence developer use case, desired tier, and target schedule.
2. **Review & preview**: We verify relevance, confirm asset permissions, and share desktop/mobile preview cards with proposed links.
3. **Written agreement**: Confirm deliverables, dates, pricing, and settlement method in writing.
4. **Publication & verification**: Following settlement confirmation, the card goes live across all four language directories and viewports with verified outbound links.
5. **Summary & completion**: Receive aggregate performance figures for the term. The card is naturally retired upon expiration unless renewal is mutually confirmed.

**Payment Rails**: Commercial placements support Stripe, WeChat Pay, Alipay, and corporate bank wire transfer. Invoicing details are provided upon written agreement. If you wish to support open-source maintenance without a commercial placement, voluntary donations via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/logicrw) are warmly welcomed.

## Editorial Independence & Transparency

Sponsorship purchases labeled, transparent display placement. It **never** buys organic ranking boosts, biased editorial endorsements, guaranteed inclusion for commercial products as open-source projects, or concealed Agent recommendations. Existing catalog entries strictly maintain identical evidence and licensing standards.

We accept factual, verifiable product claims and authorized logos only. No tracking pixels, intrusive advertising scripts, hidden affiliate tags, or unverified benchmarks are permitted. See [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md) for complete disclosure standards.

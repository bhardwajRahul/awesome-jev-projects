## Who it fits / 适合谁

Awesome Jev is an independent directory of Jev integrations and related public-code projects. Useful partners include browser and Agent infrastructure, model gateways, developer tools, evaluation services and deployment platforms. A placement should answer a concrete question: what does this tool help a developer do, and where can they try it?

Awesome Jev 是独立维护的 Jev 集成与相关公开代码目录。浏览器与 Agent 基础设施、模型网关、开发工具、评测服务和部署平台，都可以提出合作。展示内容需要说清楚一件事：它帮开发者完成什么，在哪里可以开始使用。

The catalog currently holds **259 project records**, including **252 source-reviewed entries and 7 explicitly pending review**. That is coverage, not a count of developers, customers or visitors. Source review does not establish production use, safety or performance. See the [catalog audit](docs/catalog-review-2026-09-19.md).

目前目录保留 **259 条项目记录：252 条完成源码说明核对，7 条明确标为待复核**。这是目录覆盖量，不代表开发者、客户或访客人数；源码核对也不是生产采用、性能或安全保证。详见[目录审查记录](docs/catalog-review-2026-09-19.md)。

## From inquiry to publication / 从咨询到上线

1. **Send a brief.** Share the project link, one-sentence use case, preferred dates and plan. / **发来简要需求。** 提供项目链接、一句话用途、希望的档期与方案。
2. **Review the fit and preview.** We check relevance and claims, confirm asset permissions, and share a card preview with the proposed links. / **确认契合度与预览。** 核对相关性、文案事实与素材授权，提供卡片及链接预览。
3. **Confirm the order.** Agree on deliverables, exact dates, price, payment method and cancellation or refund arrangements in writing. / **确认合作单。** 书面约定交付物、日期、金额、付款方式及取消或退款安排。
4. **Publish and verify.** After the agreed payment step, publish the approved content, check desktop/mobile and all four languages, and send the live links. / **上线与核对。** 按约定完成付款环节后，发布已确认内容，核对桌面、手机和四语展示，交付实际链接。
5. **Report and close.** Share the available aggregate measurements for the agreed period and remove the placement at the end, unless both sides confirm a renewal. / **回报与结束。** 提供约定区间内可取得的汇总数据；到期撤下，双方确认续期除外。

**Confirm first, pay second.** We support GitHub Sponsors, Stripe, WeChat Pay, and Alipay. Payment information is shared after the plan and schedule are agreed in writing.

**先沟通，后付款。** 支持 GitHub Sponsors、Stripe、微信或支付宝。双方书面确认合作方案与排期后，再提供对应的付款信息。

## Editorial independence / 收录独立

Sponsorship buys the agreed, labeled display space. It does not buy a higher organic search result, a favorable review, catalog admission, hidden Agent recommendations or search-engine ranking. A commercial product can sponsor the directory without being represented as an open-source project. Existing entries keep the same evidence and licensing standards.

赞助购买的是约定且明确标注的展示位，不是自然搜索排名、好评、收录资格、隐蔽的 Agent 推荐或搜索引擎排名。商业产品可以赞助本站，但不会被包装成开源项目；已有条目继续遵循同样的证据与许可标准。

We accept factual, relevant product information and authorized logos. No advertising scripts, third-party tracking pixels, concealed affiliate relationships or unsupported performance claims are part of these plans. The full disclosure is in [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md).

合作内容限于相关、可核查的产品信息与已授权 Logo，不包括广告脚本、第三方追踪像素、隐蔽佣金关系或无依据的性能承诺。完整披露见 [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md)。

## Measurement and privacy / 统计与隐私

Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured for aggregate visits and performance. The site loader respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Blockers and privacy preferences can reduce measured counts; page views are not verified ad impressions, distinct people or outbound conversions. Configuration is not proof that the deployment has started collecting data. Before booking, ask for actual available figures, their source and date range. No visitor-level data, public dashboard, traffic minimum or conversion guarantee is offered.

Cloudflare Web Analytics 已配置用于访问与性能汇总；站点加载器尊重浏览器的 Do Not Track 和 Global Privacy Control 信号。拦截器和隐私设置可能使统计偏低；页面访问量不等于已验证的广告可见曝光、独立人数或外链转化。配置完成不代表部署已经开始采集。合作前可索取实际可用数据、来源和时间区间；不提供访客明细、公开面板、保底流量或转化保证。
